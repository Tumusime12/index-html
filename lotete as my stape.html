About Citizen Voice

Citizen Voice is a digital platform designed to connect citizens with their leaders.


A citizen can submit a question to the responsible leader.


The leader can respond through the dashboard.


If the leader does not respond within the allowed period, the question can be escalated to a higher level of leadership.
